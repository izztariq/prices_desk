## 3. Step 4 (REFLECT): Data Scope & Limitations (~100 Words)

The provided dataset allows us to measure and compare weekly unit price movements for four specific food commodities (`Staple A`, `Oil B`, `Pulse C`, `Sweetener D`) across two locations (`City 1` and `City 2`) over a 12-week period from January to March 2026. However, the dataset cannot tell us whether overall inflation is making necessities unaffordable, nor can it reveal if consumer spending habits or purchasing power have shifted. Because the dataset consists strictly of illustrative teaching price observations restricted to four items and two cities, any conclusions drawn apply exclusively to these specific unit prices and cannot be generalized to overall consumer cost of living or official Statistical Bureau observations.
